# Questions people ask

## Where is my data kept?

In the `data` folder next to the app. Back it up by copying that folder while the app is stopped.

## Can I change the port?

Yes: start it with `myapp serve --http 0.0.0.0:3000` to listen on port 3000 on every address.

## How do I reset my password?

Choose **Forgot password** on the sign-in page and follow the link in the email.
